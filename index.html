ABOUT E-CELL

The Entrepreneurship Cell NIT Agartala is a voluntary organization fostering a vibrant start-up culture and entrepreneurial spirit. We organize dynamic events like the Startup Expo, IPL Auction, and Content Creation Conclave.

what is techtoon?

The TechToon Competition is a creative initiative by the Entrepreneurship Cell (E-Cell) of NIT Agartala, designed to blend technological concepts with visual storytelling. This competition invites participants to craft engaging and informative comics or graphic narratives that simplify complex tech or entrepreneurial ideas, making them accessible and appealing to a broader audience. Outstanding entries are showcased during E-Cell NITA events, providing creators with exposure to peers, industry professionals, and potential mentors. Winners may receive certificates, feature in E-Cell publications, and gain opportunities for networking and collaboration.

VISION

It is India's first-ever entrepreneurship cell establishment program, sowing the seeds of an entrepreneurial mindset and cultivating leadership skills in students. Aspiring to provide early awareness and exposure to the entrepreneurial world, nurturing entrepreneurship from its grassroots and laying the foundation for a thriving entrepreneurial ecosystem, propelling India into a future defined by innovation and enterprise.

RULES AND GUIDELINES

Participants must follow these rules throughout the competition.

The event is open to all NIT Agartala students from any branch and year.

Solo participation or teams of 2–3 members are allowed.(Teams preferred for better creativity).

Participants must register before the deadline to confirm their spot

Participants will receive theme-based tasks at the start of the event.

Submissions must follow the given template or format (to be shared after orientation).

Winners (Top 3 teams/individuals) will receive certificates and exciting prizes.

FAQ'S

Who can participate in the competition?

The competition is open to all NIT Agartala students, regardless of branch or year.

Can I participate alone, or do I need to form a team?

Both solo participation and teams of 2to3 members are allowed. However, teams are preferred for better creativity.

How do I register for the competition?

Participants must register before the deadline to confirm their spot.

What kind of tasks will be given in the event?

Participants must register before the deadline to confirm their spot. Registration details will be provided through official communication

What will the winners receive?

The top 3 teams or individuals will receive certificates and exciting prizes, as well as recognition for their achievements.
